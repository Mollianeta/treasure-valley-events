About Me

Hey, I'm Severin, developer of Treasure Valley Events. As someone who hsa frequently found myself totally unaware of local events in the Treasure Valley, I found myself wanting to be more in the loop. Sometimes we all can feel unsure of what we want to do with free time and want something new to try as well!

This website is my personal project to address my interest in local events and activities. Aside from just providing information on activities in the area, Treasure Valley Events aims to highlight local venues and support small businesses in the area.

As this site is made to serve the local community, suggestions or submissions are always welcome.
